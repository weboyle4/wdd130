Technical Projects & Lab Environments

Offensive Security: HackTheBox

My technical development strategy involves a dual-track approach to security mastery. I leverage HackTheBox to stay at the forefront of modern penetration testing techniques and exploit development. This platform allows me to practice real-world scenarios in a controlled environment, focusing on privilege escalation, network enumeration, and understanding the methodology of an attacker to better prepare for defensive roles.

Defensive Engineering: Proxmox Home Lab

Simultaneously, I maintain a high-availability Proxmox home lab where I engineer complex network simulations. This infrastructure allows me to bridge the gap between theoretical vulnerabilities and practical remediation by testing security controls across multiple virtualized subnets and operating systems. I regularly deploy pfSense firewalls and Ubuntu servers to practice securing traffic and managing user permissions in a sandbox environment.

Continuous Learning

I am constantly documenting my findings to improve my technical writing and reporting skills, which are crucial for any cybersecurity professional. By simulating both the "red team" (attack) and "blue team" (defense) perspectives, I develop a holistic understanding of the digital landscape. This hands-on approach ensures that I am not just learning theory, but applying security principles to real-world hardware and software configurations.
