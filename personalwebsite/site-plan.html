Content

Home Page

Welcome to my professional cybersecurity portfolio. My name is Billy Boyle, and I am a dedicated Cybersecurity student at BYU-Idaho. In an era where data is the most valuable currency, my mission is to build, defend, and optimize the digital infrastructures that keep our information safe. My journey into technology began as a curious kid finding ways around home internet restrictions. That early spark has evolved into a professional pursuit. I am currently deep-diving into offensive and defensive security, preparing for the CompTIA Security+ certification. This site serves as a live documentation of my growth and transition into a high-impact professional.

Resume

My professional background is rooted in the belief that effective security starts with a deep understanding of hardware and protocols. With my CompTIA A+ and Network+ certifications, I have built a solid mastery of troubleshooting and systems administration. I am proficient in managing Windows and Linux environments, configuring virtual machines, and implementing basic firewall rules. At BYU-Idaho, I am undergoing training in digital forensics, cryptography, and network defense. I am committed to lifelong learning, ready to contribute to a team's defensive posture in the ever-changing threat landscape.

Projects

I actively use HackTheBox to practice ethical hacking in a controlled, legal environment. These labs allow me to explore vulnerabilities like SQL injection and privilege escalation. Documentation of my "write-ups" demonstrates my ability to think like an attacker to better defend a system. Additionally, I maintain a robust home lab environment running on Proxmox. I have configured a virtualized network that includes a pfSense firewall and a Windows Server Domain Controller. This setup allows me to practice configuring Active Directory and monitoring traffic using tools like Wireshark and Snort. I use tools like Lucidchart and Nmap to visualize and scan my lab environments, ensuring nothing is left unprotected.
