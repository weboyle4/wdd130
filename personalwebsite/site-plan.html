Overview

Purpose

The primary purpose of this website is to serve as a high-fidelity evidence locker for my professional capabilities in the cybersecurity sector. In a technical field where "knowing" is secondary to "doing," this platform provides the necessary space to document the practical application of security principles that a one-page resume cannot accommodate. I plan for this site to function as a bridge between my professional credentials and my technical reality, offering a transparent look at my current projects and lab environments.

Audience

My intended audience for this platform consists primarily of potential employers, technical recruiters, and hiring managers who are looking for a deep dive into a candidate's practical abilities. I want this site to be the destination for anyone who has seen my LinkedIn profile or my resume and wants to verify the depth of my expertise before initiating an interview.

Resume Summary

To provide immediate context for visitors, I plan to include a dedicated section that summarizes my professional background and resume. This paragraph will highlight my core competencies in network security, threat analysis, and ethical hacking, while also detailing my formal education in the field. I intend to showcase my academic journey, listing relevant coursework and degrees that have provided me with a strong theoretical foundation. This summary will serve as the "executive overview" of my career.
