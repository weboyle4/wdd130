Overview

Purpose

I’m Billy Boyle, and I am currently studying cybersecurity at BYU-Idaho. Currently, I want to be in a position where I can learn more with real-world experience. I wouldn’t say I have a dream job quite yet. That is why I want to get real-world experience. Ever since I was a kid, my parents tried to stop me from using the internet, and I always found a way around it. As I grew older, I realized I could get paid to do this. As I learned more about cybersecurity, I fell in love with it. Right now, I am certified in Network+ and A+. What I am doing to learn more is getting my Security+ and doing HackTheBox. Can I connect with you on LinkedIn so we can schedule a time for an informational interview to learn more about the field?

Audience

Future employers, recruiters, and cybersecurity professionals.
